Measurements for every suit, dress shirt and pair of trousers — with a soft tape, a helper, and a reminder of exactly where the tape goes.

Where the tape goes

Pick a measurement to see the tape on the figure and the steps.

    Before you start

    • Use a soft cloth or vinyl tape; keep it flat, never twisted.
    • Measure over a thin T-shirt or dress shirt — not a sweater or jacket.
    • Snug, not tight: you should be able to slide one finger under the tape.
    • Have a helper for sleeve, shoulder and inseam; the person being measured stands relaxed and looks straight ahead.
    • Measure twice. If the numbers differ, use the larger one.
    • Growing boys: re-measure every 4–6 months, and before any suit purchase.
